    Questions

    Good to know.

    Still wondering about something? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply quickly.

    Do my guests need to download anything?

    No. Guests tap the link in WhatsApp and the invitation opens in their phone’s browser. It works on Android and iPhone, and on older phones too.

    Can I change details after sending?

    Yes. Update the time, venue or programme whenever you need to. Every guest sees the latest version on the same link, so there’s nothing to resend.

    How do I test it before paying?

    Every invitation starts in preview. You can add up to five guests, including yourself, open your personal links, reply and see the entry pass, exactly as your guests will.

    How do I pay?

    Pay for your plan with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, then tap “I’ve paid” in your dashboard. We activate your invitation, usually within the hour.

    What if a guest loses their link or pass?

    Resend it from your dashboard in a tap. At the gate, ushers can also find any guest by name and check them in.

    Can we add our own music and photos?

    Yes. Upload a song and up to twelve photos from your dashboard. Please use music you have the right to share, such as a royalty-free track.

    Is our guest list private?

    Your guest list is only visible through your private dashboard link. We never share it or use it for anything else.
